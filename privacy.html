Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

This policy describes how Catnibee Uploader (the "App") handles data. The App is a private tool operated by the owner of the Catnibee channel (@thecatnibee). It is used only to upload and publish videos created by the channel owner to the channel owner's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data the App accesses

  • OAuth access tokens that the channel owner grants to the App for their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.
  • Basic account information needed to post (such as the account ID and username of the channel owner's account).
  • The video files, titles, descriptions and settings that the channel owner chooses to upload.

The App does not access, collect or store data about any other person, including viewers, followers or commenters.

How data is used

Data is used only to upload and publish the channel owner's own content and to confirm that uploads succeeded. Data is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics about other users, or any purpose other than publishing.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and settings are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. The App does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data is sent only to the platform being posted to (Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram, TikTok) as required by their official APIs.

Google user data

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions, Instagram settings (Website permissions → Apps and websites), or TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions). Revoking access and deleting the locally stored token file removes all data held by the App.
